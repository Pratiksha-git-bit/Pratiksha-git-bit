Part 9

Recommendation: how Scalric wins

Enter as the AI and automation partner that recovers lost revenue.

  1. Pick a sharp nicheShopify D2C brands with ₹5–50 crore sales (India) or $1–20M (US), in beauty, fashion, and health and wellness.
  2. Open doors with a 7-day Revenue Leak AuditUse the store’s own data and the calculator below to show the money lost each month.
  3. Land with one productAI Revenue Recovery agent: cart recovery, 24/7 answers, COD confirmation (India) or missed-call capture (overseas).
  4. ExpandProfit dashboard and CRM, then checkout fixes, SEO for AI search, and AI-made ad creatives.
  5. Partner, don’t fightWhite-label AI agents for the 1,330+ Indian Shopify agencies that lack AI skills.
  6. Roll out over 12 monthsQ1 India pilots, Q2 case studies and partners, Q3 US launch, Q4 test UK and Canada, aiming for ~50 clients.
